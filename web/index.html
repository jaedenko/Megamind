MEGAMIND

Turns your projects into a LinkedIn post and an X post, emails you both, and posts to LinkedIn only after you say yes. You paste the X post yourself.

How it works

1. CollectMegamind reads your GitHub, Notion and website.
2. GroupIt sorts everything into your projects.
3. ScheduleYou pick when to post. On that day it writes a draft.
4. ApproveThe draft arrives by email. Reply Y to post to LinkedIn, or reply with changes.

Setup checklist

    1. Your sources

    Where Megamind finds your work. Press Update to pull in the latest. Nothing is ever changed at the source.

    SourceStatusLast updatedItems

    2. Your projects

    Megamind sorts your items into projects. Click a project to see what is in it, or fix a mistake with the dropdown next to any item.

    3. Everything collected

    Don't want something used in posts? Press Ignore. You can bring it back any time.

    Write a post

    Megamind writes a LinkedIn post and a short X post and emails both to you. Reply Y (or a thumbs-up) to publish the LinkedIn post, N for a fresh try, or reply with changes, or paste your own full version. It repeats until you say Y. After you approve, you get one more email with the X post, ready to copy and paste. Nothing is ever posted without your Y, and Megamind never posts to X.

    Schedule posts

    On the day, Megamind writes the draft and emails it to you for approval.
